Where your books live

Books and photos are saved in this browser, on this computer. Nothing is uploaded: the website only delivers the app. They stay after you close the tab, restart, or go offline. They're tied to this browser (Chrome and Edge on the same computer don't share), so use Save book file to back one up or move it to another computer.
